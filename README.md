# Hacker Role in Among Us

Design document for **the Hacker**, a new Impostor role for Among Us. It is designed as a BepInEx/Reactor mod for private lobbies where everyone has the mod installed.

👉 Read the full spec: [HACKER_ROLE_DESIGN.md](HACKER_ROLE_DESIGN.md)

| Ability | Summary |
|---|---|
| Glitch Task | Turns a player's next task into a silly variant from anywhere on the map, but tells them which room the hack came from |
| Pocket Cam | Lets the Hacker use the cameras from anywhere with a portable tablet that runs on a battery and makes the camera lights blink |
| Blue Screen | Once per game: plant a bug on a player during the round, then kill them during a meeting |
